Please Note

  • Seats are allotted subject to availability in the applied class.
  • The school may conduct an interaction or assessment as per its policy.
  • All information and documents submitted must be accurate and valid.
  • Details on transport, uniform, etc. will be provided upon confirmation.

Admission Steps

  1. Enquiry & VisitCall or drop in at the office to get forms, brochures, and have your questions answered.
  2. ApplicationComplete the form with correct details of the child and parent/guardian.
  3. DocumentsSubmit the filled form with attested copies of all required documents.
  4. Interaction / AssessmentIf required, the child will be called for a short interaction or level-appropriate assessment.
  5. Offer & FeesSelected candidates receive an offer; the seat is secured by paying the notified fees.

Key School Norms

  • Attendance must be regular and punctual.
  • Neat uniform is compulsory on all school days and official events.
  • Courtesy and respect towards staff and peers are non-negotiable.
  • Mobiles and personal gadgets are not allowed during school hours.
  • We expect parents to attend PTMs and stay in touch with the school.

Indicative Timeline

  • Forms available: Early January
  • Last date for submission: End of March
  • Interactions/assessments: February–April
  • Offers and confirmation: As announced by the school
